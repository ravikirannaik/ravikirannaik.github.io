Introduction

Macroeconomics-I is the first course in the undergraduate macroeconomics sequence at FLAME University for the 2026-27 academic year. It introduces the measurement of the aggregate economy and the core short-run models used to analyse income, employment, money, and prices, with applications to the Indian and global economy.

Part 1: Measuring the Macroeconomy

  • National income accounting and GDP measurement
  • Nominal vs. real variables, price indices, and inflation
  • Unemployment: measurement and types
  • The circular flow of income

Part 2: Aggregate Demand and the Short Run

  • Consumption, saving, and investment
  • The Keynesian cross and the multiplier
  • The IS-LM framework
  • Aggregate demand and aggregate supply

Part 3: Money and Policy

  • Money, banking, and the central bank
  • Fiscal policy and the government budget
  • Monetary policy and interest rates
  • Introduction to open-economy macroeconomics

Assessment

Assessment is through problem sets, a mid-term examination, and an end-term examination. Details and the week-by-week schedule are provided on the course LMS.
