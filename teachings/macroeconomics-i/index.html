Macroeconomics-I (ECON 202)

Undergraduate Macroeconomics-I at FLAME University, Monsoon 2026 — GDP, CPI, and unemployment; productivity and output; consumption, saving and investment; money and prices; and the IS-LM / AS-AD general-equilibrium framework.
